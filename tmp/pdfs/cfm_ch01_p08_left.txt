> 3.3. UEFA as a competition organiser and a developer and promoter of football in Europe

To become UEFA members, national associations must recognise that only UEFA has the authority to organise
international relations between football bodies in Europe (UEFA, July 2020, Article 51(1)). National associations
decide which clubs from their respective countries will be put forward to take part in UEFA’s international
competitions, but UEFA has ultimate authority over who participates (UEFA, July 2020, Article 50(1)). UEFA’s
competitions are listed in Table 1.4.
