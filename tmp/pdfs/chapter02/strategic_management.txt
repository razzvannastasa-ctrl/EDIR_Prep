Much has been written on the subject of strategic planning and analysis, both in sport and other industries.
The same basic methodologies apply to all types of organisation, even if the internal and external environments
have different dynamics, but the fundamentals on which good strategies are based remain the same: (1) a
profound understanding of the environment in which the organisation operates; (2) an objective appraisal
of the organisation’s resources and capabilities; (3) the formulation of clear goals and objectives; and (4)
effective implementation.
